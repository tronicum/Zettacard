# Bavarian (`bar`) trial translation — 12 questions for native review

Sample drawn from `fuehrerschein` across 9 topics. German is authoritative; the
Bavarian must preserve the same meaning and keep the same correct answer.

**Convention used:** legal and signage terms stay Standard German (`Zeichen 136`,
`Ortstafel`, `Vorfahrt`, `Warnblinkanlage`, `Promille`, `Alpine-Symbol`, `M+S`) because a
learner meets those exact words on real signs and in the official exam. Everything
around them is written Bairisch, moderate register — closer to bar.wikipedia than to
broad phonetic spelling. Formal *Sie* is kept where the German uses it.
